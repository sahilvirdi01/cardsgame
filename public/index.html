          How to play Bhabhi Thulla

          • Goal: empty your hand. The last player still holding cards is the Bhabhi / Thulla (loser).
          • The holder of the Ace of Spades starts by playing it.
          • The leader plays any card — its suit is what everyone must follow.
          • If you have the led suit, you must play it.
          • If you don't, play any card — that's a Thulla. The trick stops instantly and the player who played the highest card of the led suit picks up the whole pile.
          • If nobody Thullas, the cards are discarded and the highest card of the led suit leads next.
          • Empty your hand to get away safely — the last player still holding cards is the Bhabhi.
          • The host can tweak house rules in the lobby: turn direction, when escapes count, and who leads after an escape.
